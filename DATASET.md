## Limits to keep in mind

- **Visual labels.** "Sprayed-like" is a judgement from what is visible in the plot, by one observer. It is not a confirmed herbicide application.
- **One season, one region.** All points come from spring 2026, almost all from Eastern Attica.
- **Clustered sampling.** Points were collected in groups on each field day. Any model trained on them needs spatial cross-validation.
- **Points, not parcels.** Each observation is a GPS point at the plot, not a parcel boundary.
- **Andros.** The 7 points on Andros are a different landscape and should be analysed separately.

## Availability

The point data and photos are **not published yet**. They are being prepared for analysis with Sentinel-2 and PlanetScope imagery. To ask about the data or a collaboration, open an issue in this repository.

Locations of individual plots will not be published at a level of detail that could identify a farmer.
